The idea

ASKMAP Stack Concept is a proposed home for practical technology learning and digital innovation. Its vision connects education, digital solutions, entrepreneurship, mentorship, and a community where learners can build useful things together.

Its topic interests include AI, agentic workflows, digital skills, cybersecurity, and technology opportunities for African young people.

What I have shaped

  • An early mission and positioning around learning, building, connecting, and growth.
  • A possible ecosystem of educational resources, solution-building support, and community programs.
  • Exploratory content directions for AI, software, entrepreneurship, and digital capability.

These are concept-stage activities—not evidence of a launched platform, operating program, client base, or commercial success.

Principles for the idea

01 / ACCESS

Practical learning

Make abstract technology topics approachable with applied projects.

02 / CREATION

Build, not just consume

Encourage prototypes and solutions linked to everyday problems.

03 / CONNECTION

Community support

Create space for learners to share progress and discover collaborators.

04 / SUSTAINABILITY

Validate demand first

Explore learner needs and an achievable initial offering before scaling.

Next steps before launch

Interview prospective learners, narrow the first audience, validate one valuable learning or community program, develop small educational pilots, and measure genuine participation before presenting ASKMAP as an established ecosystem.

The goal is not to make a big claim today, but to show clear thinking about a venture worth testing.
