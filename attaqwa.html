The context

At Attaqwa Laundry & Dry Cleaning Services, the work goes beyond providing a cleaning service. Each order requires accurate intake, careful handling, clear customer communication, and reliable coordination until garments are returned.

That practical environment has shaped my interest in designing systems that make work easier for customers, teams, and delivery partners.

What I do

  • Manage daily customer communication and service requests.
  • Coordinate orders, garment-care standards, and internal workflows.
  • Support inventory handling, pickup and delivery coordination, and service follow-up.
  • Work with staff and service processes to improve reliability and organization.
  • Look for recurring operational friction that could be addressed with better records, interfaces, and communication.

The role demonstrates ownership, follow-through, and familiarity with operational constraints; it is not presented as software engineering employment.

Problems worth solving

01 / RECORDS

Item-level clarity

Garment-level information can help staff and customers agree on what was handed over.

02 / COMMUNICATION

Fewer status questions

Timely progress updates could reduce avoidable back-and-forth communication.

03 / HANDOFFS

Delivery coordination

Clear roles, pickup details, and handover confirmation matter at every transition.

04 / CONSISTENCY

Repeatable workflows

Simple processes help teams give customers a more predictable experience.

From operations to product thinking

The biggest lesson from operating a service is that technology should support the actual sequence of work—not impose an idealized workflow. This perspective informs the Tranq concept, especially order tracking, garment identification, delivery visibility, and trust-building communication.

An interface is only useful when it respects what the people doing the work actually need.

Evidence and limitations

The operational responsibilities described here are drawn from my professional CV. This portfolio does not claim a certain number of customers, employees, orders, branches, or years in business, because those numbers have not been verified.
