Who I am

I turn everyday service problems into digital opportunities.

I am a product and UI/UX design practitioner with growing web and AI engineering skills. My thinking is shaped by practical experience running Attaqwa Laundry & Dry Cleaning Services, where I have worked on customer service, operations, garment handling, order coordination, and service delivery.

That real-world experience inspired Tranq, a smart laundry and garment-tracking concept designed to bring visibility, trust, and better coordination to laundry services in Nigeria.

I am currently building a realistic portfolio that reflects both what I have already done and what I am actively learning, especially in web development, product design, and AI engineering.
