# Abdulhakeem Temitope Akinola — Professional Portfolio

A responsive, offline-ready multi-page professional portfolio based on the user's professional CV and documented project conversations. Prepared October 2026.

## Files

- `index.html`: homepage, projects, about, experience, skills, contact
- `tranq.html`: research-led Tranq UX definition case study
- `attaqwa.html`: actual Attaqwa Laundry operations experience
- `assistant.html`: early-stage Tranq AI assistant and planned AI Engineering capstone
- `askmap.html`: ASKMAP Stack Concept (explicitly labeled idea stage)
- `style.css`: complete visual styling
- `script.js`: responsive mobile menu, copy email, dynamic copyright year

## Open and publish

Open `index.html` in your browser. No install, paid services, build tools, or external libraries are needed.

For a live site, upload the **five HTML pages plus style.css and script.js in one folder** to a static hosting provider (e.g., Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages). Set `index.html` as the homepage. This package does **not** mean a live site has already been published.

## Important personalizations before publishing

1. **Contact:** The site currently uses the Attaqwa Laundry business email found in the CV. Replace it with your preferred professional email in the HTML pages and the `data-copy` button if needed. Phone numbers have deliberately been omitted from the public website.
2. **Evidence:** Attach actual approved screenshots of your Figma work, design documents, prototypes, and any live deployed projects. Current graphics are explicitly conceptual to avoid presenting a mockup as a product screenshot.
3. **Verified links:** Add LinkedIn, GitHub, a public Figma prototype, or a CV download only once you have confirmed their URLs and sharing permissions. None have been invented.
4. **Dates:** Update the AI Engineering curriculum status once begun, and only add completed deliverables with proof.
5. **Case studies:** Add accurate methods, evidence, usability feedback, and measured results only when documented and verifiable. Do not claim tested outcomes or commercial success based solely on concepts.
6. **Attaqwa visuals:** Add approved images if you wish, but protect customers' privacy.

## Accuracy commitments

The site distinguishes between (a) actual professional service operations, (b) documented Tranq definition artifacts, (c) developing/early-stage design and knowledge-base plans, (d) ASKMAP at the idea stage, and (e) the planned 20 Oct 2026 to 20 Jan 2027 AI Engineering curriculum. It does not imply a formal degree, shipped Tranq product, production AI agent, quantified business traction, or unverified FlyRank completion.

## Editing

Everything is regular HTML/CSS/JS. You can open in VS Code and edit text between the tags. Colors/typography/layout are controlled in `style.css`. The project cards in `index.html` point to the matching case study files.
